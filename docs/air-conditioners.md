# Air conditioners

**English** · [Français](air-conditioners.fr.md)

An air conditioner can heat in winter and cool in summer, in the same week. On an air
conditioner, each slot says what the device does, for example *Cool*. The preset then says how
warm, for example *Comfort* at 25 °C when cooling.

<a id="the-hvac-mode-first-the-air-conditioners"></a>

## Choose what the device does in a slot

Open a slot of an air conditioner, then make two choices.

1. **What the device does.** Choose an HVAC mode, for example *Heat*, *Cool* or *Off*. The
   thermostat switches to this HVAC mode when the slot starts.
2. **How warm.** Choose a preset, for example *Comfort*. In *Cool*, Chauffage sends the
   preset's temperature when cooling.

*Off*, *Dry*, *Auto* and *Fan only* have no preset.

**How warm when cooling.** You set each preset's temperature when cooling in the *Presets* tab.
[Presets](presets.md#what-a-preset-does-on-each-thermostat) explains the two temperatures of a
preset.

**Heat/Cool.** A *Heat/Cool* slot keeps the room between the preset's two temperatures, for
example between 20 °C and 25 °C. Chauffage offers *Heat/Cool* on a thermostat that takes a range
of temperatures.

**A whole week.** To make a whole week heat or cool, open the ⋮ menu of a day of the thermostat,
then tap *All heat* or *All cool*. The heating and cooling slots of the week take this HVAC
mode. The other slots stay as they are, for example an *Off* slot. Tap *Save* to keep the
change.

**Colors.** To change the color of a preset when cooling, open the *Presets* tab.

**Summer and winter.** Create a *Summer* schedule as a copy of your *Winter* schedule, and set
its slots to *Cool*. To change season, activate the other schedule. You can activate it by hand,
from an automation, or with an [activation rule](activation-rules.md).

**Switch an air conditioner by hand.** Switch an air conditioner to *Cool* during a slot that
heats. This change is an [override](overrides.md). The air conditioner keeps its own setpoint.
To cool at the slot's preset, tap *Cool* on the card instead. The air conditioner then takes the
preset's temperature when cooling.

## Fan only, dry and auto

On a thermostat that offers them, **Fan only**, **Dry** and **Auto** are HVAC modes of a slot.

- A *Fan only* slot runs the fan without heating or cooling.
- A *Dry* slot dehumidifies.
- An *Auto* slot lets the device choose between heating and cooling.

**Fan and vanes.** On an air conditioner, open *More settings* in a slot to choose the fan speed
and the vanes for this slot. Leave the first choice to set nothing.
